Agenda item

2026/27 Budget and Medium Term Financial Strategy

  • Meeting of Cabinet, Wednesday, 11 February 2026 5.00 pm (Item 8.)
  • View the background to item 8.

Minutes:

RESOLVED:

 

That Cabinet:

 

Revenue Budget

 

1.       Approved total General Fund savings of £20.5m over the MTFS period 2026/27 to 2029/30, of which £11.9m is to be delivered from the General Fund in 2026/27 (Appendix 2).

 

2.       Following full Council approval of each proposal, authorises the Strategic Director with responsibility for each proposal (including fees and charges) to:

a)           carry out all necessary steps to implement each proposal, including any necessary consultation.

b)           consider any consultation outcomes and other implications.

c)           complete and consider the implications of any equalities analysis assessment required.

d)           following the above:

i)            determine whether to amend any proposal

ii)           determine whether a further report needs to be considered by the relevant decision maker before implementation

iii)          where a decision is taken not to proceed, bring forward alternative proposal(s).

                                                                                

3.       Approved £30.4m of revenue growth for pressures and investments in 2026/27 which will be included in departmental control totals used for service budget setting.

 

4.       Noted the budget proposals are presented in advance of the publication of the Final Local Government Finance Settlement, and delegates any amendments to the budget proposals as a result of changes in the final settlement to the Strategic Director, Resources in consultation with the Leader and the Cabinet Member for Inclusive Economy.

 

 

REASONS FOR DECISION AND OPTIONS CONSIDERED:

 

1.       The purpose of this report on the 2026/27 Budget and Medium-Term Financial Strategy (MTFS) is to enable Cabinet to consider the budget proposals and make recommendations to Full Council when it finalises the budget and sets council tax on 3 March 2026.

2.       The option not to consider the budget and council tax proposals was not considered as the council is legally required to set a balanced budget and council tax each financial year.

3.       The council has continued to provide budget growth in services that experience significant and continued demand and market pressures, with prioritisation being given to the most vulnerable groups. Due to the complexity of the service provision and notwithstanding the increase in cash-terms in funding in the Provisional Local Government Finance Settlement, these services continue to operate in a challenging resource environment where even small demand changes can lead to material budget variances.

4.       The savings proposals in this report align with the strategic vision, Council Plan and the MTFS on the allocation of resources. Some of the savings proposals may have further implications which will only emerge following detailed planning and consultation. Where this is the case, those implications will be considered before a final decision is taken on implementation, including whether a proposal should be amended. Where detailed proposals result in a lower financial saving, it is the responsibility of the relevant Strategic Director to find alternative replacement savings.

 

Cllr Mason on behalf of Cabinet, put on record his thanks to officers for working together with the administration to bring together a historic no-cuts budget the first in fifteen years.
