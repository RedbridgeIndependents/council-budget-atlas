Information about Full Council

Full Council consists of all 50 elected councillors and usually meets five times a year to take major budgetary and policy framework decisions. The Council must consider certain high-level strategic plans, including the Crime and Disorder Reduction Strategy, Community Strategy, and Local Development Framework. In February Full Council agrees the annual revenue and capital budgets which sets the level of Council Tax for the forthcoming Municipal Year.

 

The full terms of reference and procedure rules for Full Council can be found in our constitution on this page:

www.lbhf.gov.uk/councillors-and-democracy/councillors-committees-and-decisions

 

Members of the public can ask questions about council business and policy. More information about public questions can be found on this page:

www.lbhf.gov.uk/councillors-and-democracy/councillors-committees-and-decisions/take-part-democratic-process
