Decision details

Council Budget 2026/27

  • Find out more about this issue

Decision Maker: Mayor and Cabinet

Decision status: Recommendations Approved

Is Key decision?: Yes

Is subject to call in?: No

Decision:

RESOLVED:

Mayor and Cabinet 

 

General Fund Revenue Budget

1.     notes and asks Council to note the 2025/26 forecast projected overall variance of £1.50m underspend, after the budgeted use of Corporate Provisions and Reserves against the 2025/26 budget of £312.515m as set out in Section 6 of this report, and that this year-end underspend will result in a commensurate reduction in the draw on reserves;

2.     endorses and asks Council to endorse the budget cut reduction measures of £0.821m (previously agreed) and £29.249m (agreed in 2025/26), for 2026/27 as set out in Section 6 of the report and summarised in Appendices Y2 and Y3 respectively;

3.     agrees and asks Council to agree the allocation of £53.503m of resources from Core Spending Power and Reserves in 2026/27 to be invested in funding quantified budget pressures and opportunities, both recurring and once-off, as set out in Section 6;

4.     agrees to recommend to Council that a General Fund Budget Requirement of £422.220m for 2026/27 be approved;

5.     asks Council to agree to a 4.99% increase in Lewisham’s Council Tax element. This will result in a Band D equivalent Council Tax level of £1,726.82 for Lewisham’s services and £2,237.33 overall. This represents an overall increase in Council Tax for 2026/27 of 4.79% and is subject to the Greater London Authority (GLA) precept for 2026/27 being increased by £20.13 (i.e. 4.10%) from £490.38 to £510.51, in line with the GLA’s draft budget proposals;

6.     notes the final Fair Funding Allocation and grants within Core Spending Power for 2026/27 has not yet been announced and agrees to delegate authority to the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources to include any change to the provisional settlement of £260,316,192 in the Budget report for Council with any difference to the position set out in this report covered from Corporate Provisions and Reserves as necessary;

7.     notes the provisional and estimated precept and levies from the GLA and other bodies as detailed in Appendix Y9 and agrees to delegate authority to the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources to include any changes once confirmed in the Budget report for Council with any difference covered from provisions and reserves as necessary;

8.     notes and asks Council to note the Council Tax Ready Reckoner which, for illustrative purposes, sets out the Band D equivalent Council Tax at various levels of increase. This is explained in Section 6 of the report with more detail in Appendix Y7;

9.     asks that the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources issues cash limits to all Directorates and publishes the Budget Book for 2026/27 for the record once the 2026/27 Revenue Budget is agreed;

10. considers, and asks Council to consider, the Section 25 Statement from the Chief Finance Officer, the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources. This is attached at Appendix Y8;

11. agrees and asks Council to agree the draft statutory calculations for 2026/27 as set out at Appendix Y9;

12. notes and asks Council to note the prospects for the revenue budget for 2026/27 and future years, including the planned use of £2.442m of reserves to enable the setting of a balanced budget;

13. notes, and asks Council to note, the updated Medium Term Financial Strategy and associated need for at least £50m of budget reductions, to be agreed and implemented in the period 2026/27 to 2028/29 to remove this reliance on reserves and meet future pressures as set out in Sections 6 and 7;

14. agrees and asks officers to continue to develop firm proposals to redesign and transform services and inform the capital strategy by bringing them forward in good time to support the work towards a savings and investment round to help plan early and meet the future forecast medium-term finance strategy objectives;

15. agrees to write-off historic Council Tax Arrears (including court costs) totalling £12,063,458.78, as set out in Appendix Y12;

16. agrees to write-off 20 cases of Former Tenants’ Arrears related to Temporary Accommodation stock totalling £346,896.56, as set out in Appendix Y12;

17. agrees to write off one case of Adult Social Care Sundry Debt Arrears totalling £170,543.92, as set out in Appendix Y12;

18. agrees to write-off one case of Business Rates Arrears totalling £144,551.11, as set out in Appendix Y12;

 

Other Grants (within the General Fund)

19. notes and asks Council to note the adjustments to and impact of various specific grants for 2026/27 on the General Fund as set out in Section 6 of this report;

 

Fees and Charges

20. approves the approach to setting 2026/27 fees and charges for chargeable services in Section 6 of the report and attached at Appendix Y10;

 

Dedicated Schools Grant and Pupil Premium

Schools Block

21. notes, and recommends that Council notes, the provisional gross Dedicated Schools Grant (DSG) allocation for 2026/27 of £418.82m before the Department for Education’s (DfE’s) adjustments to fund Academy schools;

22. notes, and recommends that Council notes, the provisional DSG allocation for £263.55m be the Schools’ Budget (Schools Block) for 2026/27 covering both maintained schools and academies, and that this includes streamlining of grants for teachers pay and NICs (2025/26 award) totalling £6.51m;

23. notes an overall increase in pupil unit funding for primary and secondary schools (after allowing for the streamlining of the teacher related grant funding as noted in 2.24). However, there has been an overall reduction of 700 pupils, 664 in primary and 36 secondary school equating to a loss of approximately £5m;

24. notes that following the Schools Forum meeting on 15 January 2026, submission has been made to the DfE recommending the application of the National Funding Formula (including unit values) allowing for a -0.5% (minus) Minimum Funding Guarantee (MFG);

25. agrees and recommends that Council agrees, as recommended by Schools Forum, the once-off transfer of circa £0.68m of Schools Block funding to the High Needs Block, a 0.26% transfer;

 

Central Schools Services Block (CSSB)

26. notes, and asks Council to note, the construct and allocation of £2.80m for the Central Schools Services Block allocation for 2026/27, a continued reduction of £0.16m (20%), plus formula led element increase of £0.04m, which is rolled in funding and not new funding;

 

High Needs Block (HNB)

27. notes, and asks Council to note, the provisional High Needs Block of £89.21m to support the Council’s statutory duty with regards to Special Education Needs (SEN), a net increase of £5.10m relative to 2025/26 (6.1%). And that the High Needs Block will be finalised in March for deductions arising for institutions funded by the Department for Education (DfE);

28. notes that the High Needs Block is expected to overspend by £2.5m in 2025/26, increasing the cumulative overspend to £21.0m in total when prior year overspends are included. Further guidance from Government is awaited on the treatment of DSG deficits as part of the long-term solution to the national accounting override issued by the DfE;

 

Early Years Block (EYB)

29. notes, and asks Council to note, the DSG allocation to the Early Years Block of £63.26m;

30. notes, and asks Council to note, that the DfE has increased hourly funding for 3- and 4-year-olds from £7.52 to £8.08 (7.4%) and with respect to 2-year-old disadvantaged funding from £11.04 to £11.47 (3.9%);

31. notes, and asks Council to note, the increased hourly funding for under 2- year-olds from £15.06 to £15.62 (3.7%);

32. notes, and asks Council to note, the increased hourly rate for 2-year-old working parent entitlement from £11.04 to £11.47 per hour (3.9%);

 

Pupil Premium

33. notes, and asks Council to note, that the pupil premium will continue in 2026/27 and that rates have increased on average by 2.3%;

34. notes that the DfE has advised a new grant will be provided to support Schools for the expansion of the Free Schools Meals entitlement, however no details have been provided as yet;

 

Housing Revenue Account

35. notes, and asks Council to note, the consultation report on proposed service charge increases to tenants and leaseholders in the Brockley area, presented to tenants and leaseholders on 3 December 2025, as attached at Appendices X2a and X2b;

36. notes, and asks Council to note, the consultation report on proposed service charge increases to tenants presented to the remaining tenants as attached at Appendix X3. Leaseholders were asked for their views at the Leasehold Forum on 11th December 2025 and a copy of the minutes are attached at Appendix X1c;

37. agrees, and asks Council to agree, to set an increase in dwelling rents of 4.80% (an average of £5.95 per week) – as per the Government formula for rent increases to be applied to rents for 2026/27 and as outlined in Section 9 of this report, and following confirmation of the delay to the implementation of a convergence element until 2027/28;

38. agrees, and asks Council to agree, to set an increase in the hostels accommodation charge of 4.80% (or £4.52 per week), in accordance with Government direction for formula rent increases to be applied to rents for 2026/27, and following confirmation of the delay to the implementation of a convergence element until 2027/28;

39. approves, and asks Council to approve, the following average weekly increases/decreases for dwellings for:

40. service charges to PFI managed dwellings (Brockley) to ensure full cost recovery and 5.50% inflationary uplift for 2026/27:

 caretaking 5.50% (£0.21)

 grounds maintenance 5.50% (£0.11)

 communal lighting 5.50% (£0.10)

 bulk waste collection 5.50% (£0.12)

 window cleaning 5.50% (£0.00)

 tenants’ levy 0.00% (£0.00)

41. service charges to Council managed dwellings to ensure full cost recovery and 3.80% inflationary uplift for 2026/27:

 caretaking 3.72% (£0.26)

 grounds maintenance 3.93% (£0.12)

 window cleaning 24.81% (£0.02)

 communal lighting 39.12 (£1.35)

 block pest control 26.36% (£0.57)

 waste collection 3.36% (£0.03)

 heating and hot water 7.70% (£1.23)

 tenants’ levy 0.00% (£0.00)

 bulk waste disposal 3.36% (£0.03)

 sheltered housing 5.00% (£1.00)

42. approves, and asks Council to approve, the following average weekly percentage changes for hostels and shared temporary units for:

43. service charges (hostels) – caretaking etc.; 5.98% or £5.09pw, energy cost increases for heat, light and power; 10.00% or £1.67pw;

44. water charges increase; 10.00% or £0.03pw;

45. approves, and asks Council to approve, an increase in garage rents by 4.50% (£0.94 per week) for Brockley PFI and Council residents as outlined in Appendix X4;

46. notes, and asks Council to note, that the budgeted expenditure for the Housing Revenue Account (HRA) for 2026/27 is £275.73m, split £127.02m revenue and £148.71m capital, which includes the decent homes and new build programmes;

47. agrees the HRA Business Plan for 2026/27, as attached at Appendix X7;

48. notes the 2025/26 Period 8 HRA Capital Programme monitoring position and the Capital Programme potential future schemes and resources as set out in Section 11 of this report;

49. agrees to write-off eight cases of Former Tenants’ Arrears related to HRA stock as set out in Appendix X5, totalling £121,783.96;

 

Treasury Management Strategy

50. approves and recommend that Council approves the Treasury Management Strategy 2026/27 including the prudential indicators and treasury indicators, as set out in Section 10 of this report;

51. approves and recommend that Council approves the Annual Investment Strategy and Creditworthiness Policy, set out in further detail at Appendix Z2, and that it formally delegates responsibility for managing transactions with those institutions which meet the criteria to the Executive Director of Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources;

52. approves and recommend that Council approves the Investment Strategy as set out in Section 10 of this report, along with the operational boundary and authorised limit for the year as set out in Tables E4 and E5;

53. approves and recommend that Council approves the Minimum Revenue Provision (MRP) policy as set out in Section 10 of this report;

54. approves and recommend that Council approves the overall credit and counterparty risk management criteria, as set out at Appendix Z2, the proposed countries for investment at Appendix Z5, and that it formally delegates responsibility for managing transactions with those institutions meeting the criteria to the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources;

55. agrees and recommend that Council agree to delegate to the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources, the authority during 2026/27 to make amendments to borrowing and investment limits provided they are consistent with the strategy and there is no change to the Council’s authorised limit for borrowing;

56. notes the change from a single pool to a two-pool borrowing structure for the General Fund and HRA as set out in Section 10 of this report;

 

Capital Programme

57. notes the 2025/26 Capital Programme position and agrees, and recommends that Council agrees, the reprofiled and amended 2025/26 Capital Programme of £177.88m as set out in Section 11 of the report;

58. agrees, and recommends that Council agrees, that any programme slippage in 2025/26 be added to the 2026/27 Capital Programme project budgets and reported in 2026/27;

59. recommends that Council approves the 2026/27 to 2029/30 Capital Programme of £539.25m as set out in Section 11 of this report;

60. agrees, and recommends that Council agrees, the Capital Strategy set out in Appendix W1;

61. agrees and recommends that Council agrees, to delegate authority to the Executive Director for Finance, Digital and Corporate Resources to amend the scheme mix within the Capital programme to respond to the risks as set out as long as this is done within the total programme parameters approved and that regular updates on the capital programme, reflecting any changes, are regularly reported back to Mayor and Cabinet;

 

Climate Budget 2026/27 – 2028/29

62. agrees the Council’s Climate Budget 2026/27 – 2028/29 as set out in Section 12 and Appendix V1.

 

63. Further to the Mayor & Cabinet decision of 10th September endorsing the Lewisham Homes Ltd Business Plan, that Mayor & Cabinet authorises the Director of Finance to renegotiate the terms of the loans to Lewisham Homes PROVIDED THAT:

(a) the total value of the loans does not exceed the current £40m total, and,

(b) the Director of Law & Corporate Governance approves the form of any revised or new agreements and that all legal and/or governance matters are complied with.

 

 

 

 

Report author: Katharine Nidd

Publication date: 12/02/2026

Decided at meeting: 11/02/2026 - Mayor and Cabinet
