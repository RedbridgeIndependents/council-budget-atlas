Funding and spending

More about our different income sources, and where this money is spent across our different service areas.

Funding and income

Image

Our current total budget for the year 2026/27 is £952million. This does not include funding from the Housing Revenue Account that covers our social housing stock.

Our funding comes from various sources, broken down as:

  • 51% from rent, sales, fees and charges
  • 20% from the Government Finance Settlement, which includes our retained business rates
  • 19% of other government funding
  • 8% from Council Tax income
  • 2% One off use of our reserves

How we spend and invest our resources

Image

We fund services across a number of areas. This spending breaks down as:

  • 28% on schools, children's social care and youth services
  • 20% on waste, street cleansing, highways and public protection
  • 19% on public health and adult social care
  • 17% on housing, temporary accommodation and rough sleeping
  • 13% on our enabling services, for example finance, policy, HR and IT
  • 3% on regeneration, planning and place shaping
