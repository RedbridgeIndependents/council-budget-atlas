Agenda item

Medium Term Financial Strategy 2026-2030 and Detailed Budget 2026/27

  • Meeting of Budget Council, Council, Thursday 26 February 2026 8:00 pm (Item 6.)
  • View the background to item 6.

A.   At its meeting on 12 February 2026, the Corporate and Resources Committee considered the report which is attached as Appendix B.

 

B.   The Corporate & Resources Committee received late material in relation to Annex 03 of the Budget Pack - ‘Statutory Council Tax Calculation 2026-27’, which is updated in the Budget Pack supplement to this agenda.

 

C.   The Corporate and Resources Committee recommendations were as follows [References to ‘the report’ are to the report at Appendix B on this agenda.] Other references are to the updated version of the ‘Budget Pack - Medium Term Financial Strategy 2026-2030 and Detailed Budget 2026/27’ which has been circulated to all Members as a separate supplement. In making its recommendations to Council the recommendations of the People Committee and Place committee were noted and are set as follows.

 

1.    Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the budget proposals and Council Tax for 2026/27 as set out in the Budget Report Pack report and annexes;

 

2.    Council be RECOMMENDED to authorise the relevant officers to take necessary action to deliver the agreed budget and service charges, including increasing relevant fees and charges in line with inflation or as recommended within budget proposals;

 

3.    Council be RECOMMENDED, in accordance with the requirements of the Local Government Act 2003, to note the comments of the Section 151 Officer in regard to the robustness of estimates and the level of reserves and balances as set out in the section “S25 Statement of Section 151 Officer” in paragraph 40 of the Budget Report Pack;

 

4.    Council be RECOMMENDED in accordance with sections 32 to 36 of the Local Government Act 1992, to calculate the amounts in the statutory Council Tax calculation (Annex 3 of the Budget Report pack) for the financial year 2026/27;

 

5.    Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the Capital Strategy including the detailed capital programme for 2026/27 to 2029/30 as set out in Annex 11 to the Budget Report;

 

6.    Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the Treasury Strategy including the Annual Investment Strategy, prudential indicators and the Minimum Revenue Provision (MRP) policy as set out in Annex 12 to the Budget Report;

 

7.    Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the pay policy at Annex 13 to the budget report;

8.    Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the Schools Budget for 2026/27 be approved in line with paragraph 20 and Enclosure 1 of Annex 14 to the Budget Pack;

 

9.    Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the proposals outlined for the Central School Services Block be approved (ref. para 31) of Annex 14 to the Budget Pack;

 

10. Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the proposals outlined for the Early Years Block be approved (ref. paras. 32-38) of Annex 14 to the Budget Pack;

 

11. Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the proposals outlined for the High Needs Block be approved (ref paras. 38-42) of Annex 14 to the Budget Pack;

 

12. Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the proposals outlined for the Schools Block (ref. paras 23-30) of Annex 14 to the Budget Pack and the Schools Funding Formula outlined in Enclosure 2 of Annex 14 and the Schools Allocations in Enclosure 3 Annex 14 be approved, including a Minimum Funding Guarantee of +0.0% per pupil (ref. paras 25-27).

 

13. Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the HRA 2025-26 base budget and proposal for growth and savings as set out in Annex 10;

 

14. Council be RECOMMENDED to approve the indicative HRA revenue budgets for 2025-26 to 2027-28 set out in Annex 10, Enclosure 3; and

 

15. Council be RECOMMENDED to approve full HRA Capital Programme as described in Annex 10, detailed in Enclosure 5;

 

D.   Council is therefore RECOMMENDED to consider and approve the recommendations as set out in items 1 to 15 above.

 

 

Minutes:

The Council considered the Medium Term Financial Strategy 2026-2030 and the 2026/27 Detailed Budget, as recommended by the Corporate and Resources Committee. Introducing the report, Councillor Richard Thorpe (Portfolio Holder for Finance) highlighted a backdrop of rising demand for social care and a £1bn funding gap across London authorities.

 

The 2026/27 budget included:

£13.43m for service growth and demand (a £3.93m increase over 2025/26).

£1.71m in new revenue savings, totalling £3.43m when combined with previous commitments.

An average Council tax band of £2,097.61 at Band D (comprising a 2.99% general increase and a 2% Adult Social Care precept).

£7.60m allocated to reserves for inflation with the General Fund balance maintained at £19.63m.

A four-year Capital Programme investment of £248m (£142.93m General Fund; £105.07m HRA).

 

 

The Leader of the Opposition, Councillor James Giles, proposed two amendments as set out in the Late Material Pack (3). These were dealt with individually in accordance with the Council’s procedural rules.

 

Amendment 1 – moved by Councillor Giles and seconded by Councillor Chohan

 

In introducing the amendment, Councillor Giles outlined concerns regarding the impact of permitted development rights on the conversion of family homes into Houses in Multiple Occupation (HMOs).

 

By way of response, the Leader of the Council, Councillor Andreas Kirsch confirmed that the issue was already being addressed through existing planning policy and officer workstreams. Consequently, Councillor James Giles withdrew his amendment.

 

Amendment 2 - moved by Councillor Giles and seconded by Councillor Chohan

 

In introducing the amendment, Councillor Giles explained that it sought to reallocate funding within the proposed budget in order to prioritise core local services. Councillor Giles expressed concern about council tax increases and the financial pressures facing residents during the ongoing cost-of-living challenges.

 

By way of response, Members emphasised the complexity of the Council’s financial position, including the need to manage borrowing costs, maintain statutory services and plan for future financial sustainability. The debate also referenced the national funding environment for local government and the wider financial pressures affecting councils across England.

 

Following the debate, Councillor Giles requested a roll call vote under Procedure Rule 26 of the Constitution. The Mayor put the amendment to the vote and it was LOST.

(35)

 

Abstentions: Councillors Noel Hadjimichael and Sue Ansari. (2)

 

The Council returned to debate on the substantive motion. Councillor Andreas Kirsch spoke in support of the proposals. He highlighted the Council’s efforts to maintain financial stability despite significant external pressures and referenced positive external assessments of the Council’s services. 

 

Councillor Kirsch and other Members expressed appreciation for the work undertaken by officers in preparing the budget and managing the Council’s finances. It was noted that local authorities across the country were facing substantial financial challenges and that the proposed budget sought to ensure the continued delivery of services to residents while maintaining a balanced financial position.
