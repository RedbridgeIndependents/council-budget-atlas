Decision details

Final Budget Proposals 2026/27 and Medium Term Financial Strategy

  • Find out more about this issue

Decision Maker: Executive

Decision status: Recommendations Approved

Is Key decision?: Yes

Is subject to call in?: Yes

Decision:

Recommendations: 

The Executive are asked to approve the following recommendations

1.1.          To agree a £7.193m transfer to the Corporate Projects and Systems reserve on 31/03/2025 following the Audited Statement of Accounts. (Paragraph 4.21)

The Executive is asked to note and endorse the following recommendations which will be referred to Full Council for approval:

The General Fund Medium-Term Financial Strategy (Section 3)

1.2.          To agree and recommend to Full Council the proposed 2026/27 Budget and Medium-Term Financial Strategy (MTFS), the financial strategy principles, and budget assumptions. (Paragraphs 3.1-3.89, Table 2 and Appendix A)

1.3.          To agree and recommend to Full Council the proposed 2026/27 net budgets by directorate. (Paragraph 2.9, Table 1 and Appendix A)

1.4.          To agree and recommend to Full Council the 2026/27 savings and note that individual savings may be subject to individual consultation before the savings can be implemented. (Paragraphs 3.80-3.83, Table 10 and, Appendices B1 and B2)

1.5.          To note the Local Government Finance Settlement 2026/27 including the Fair Funding Review 2.0 outcome, and other related funding announcements. (Paragraphs 3.11-3.31, Tables 4A and 4B)

1.6.          To agree and recommend to Full Council that the Section 151 Officer be delegated responsibility for any technical budget adjustments required for the 2026/27 budget (in line with the council’s Financial Regulations).

1.7.          To agree and recommend to Full Council that centrally held demand growth is allocated to directorate budgets in-year, only once a more evidenced assessment is available and has been approved by the Section 151 Officer.

General Fund Contingency and Reserves (Section 4) 

1.8.          To note and recommend to Full Council the forecast movements to/from General Fund reserves. (Appendix C and Paragraphs 4.14-4.22)

1.9.          To note the Balance Sheet Analysis to support the MTFS. (Paragraphs 4.33-4.35 and Appendix F1)

1.10.      To note the assessment of compliance against the CIPFA Financial Management (FM) Code. (Paragraphs 4.28-4.32 and Appendix F2)

The Housing Revenue Account Budget and Medium-Term Financial Strategy (Section 5) 

1.11.      To agree and recommend to Full Council the balanced 2026/27 HRA and note the latest estimates over the three-year MTFS period. (Table 14 and Appendix D1).

1.12.      To agree the 2026/27 savings and note that individual savings may be subject to individual consultation before they can be implemented. (Table 15 and Appendix D3)

1.13.      To agree and recommend to Full Council the HRA 30-year business plan. (Paragraph 5.2 and Appendix D4)

Capital Investment and Treasury and Investment Management (Section 6) 

1.14.      To agree and recommend to Full Council, the proposed 2025/26 to 2030/31 capital programme and indicative capital programme for 2031/32 to 2035/36. (Paragraphs 6.1-6.12, Tables 20-21, Appendix E1 and Appendix E2)

1.15.      To agree and recommend to Full Council the funding of the 2025/26 to 2035/36 capital programme and to delegate authority to the Section 151 Officer, where necessary, to apply capital resources to fund the capital programme in the most cost-effective way for the council. (Paragraphs 6.13-6.25 and Table 22)

1.16 To agree and recommend to Full Council the expenditure budgets for Open Market Sale house building schemes (Paragraphs 6.26-6.29 and Table 25) which will be funded by capital receipts (Table 25) upon their disposal.

1.17          To note that inclusion of a scheme in the capital programme does not in itself necessarily grant permission to commit expenditure against the approved capital budget. This remains subject to any additional approval requirements of the council’s constitution, financial regulations and procurement rules.

1.18          To agree and recommend to Full Council the Capital and Investment Strategy, Treasury Management Strategy and Minimum Revenue Provision (MRP) Policy Statement. (Appendices E3, E4 and E5)

1.19          To note that, further to the Executive delegation to the Corporate Director of Community Wealth Building on 20 April 2023, to enter into any required documentation with Newlon Housing Trust to facilitate Newlon’s proposed redevelopment of the Barnsbury Estate free from third party rights, that Newlon has incorporated Access Homes Housing Association Limited (Access Homes). Access Homes is a wholly owned subsidiary as a special purpose vehicle for the redevelopment of the Barnsbury Estate and Newlon has transferred legal title to the Estate to Access Homes. Therefore, save for the cost indemnity agreement in the Council’s favour which will still be provided by Newlon Housing Trust, documentation relating to the acquisition and transfer back of the Estate will now be with Access Homes.

Council Tax and Retained National Non-Domestic Rates (Section 8) 

1.20          To agree and recommend to Full Council the calculations required for the determination of 2026/27 council tax requirement and the level of council tax as detailed in Section 8 and summarised below:

1.20.1    The 2026/27 council tax requirement of £132,331,227. (Paragraph 8.5 and Table 28)

1.20.2    The relevant basic amount of Islington Band D council tax of 1,597.64, a 4.99% increase compared to 2025/26 (comprising 2% specifically for expenditure on adult social care and 2.99% for all expenditure), and that this is not ‘excessive’ in accordance with the council tax referendum principles for 2026/27. (Paragraph 8.6)

1.20.3    The basic amount of Islington Band D council tax for dwelling to which no special items relates (i.e. outside of the Lloyd Square Garden Committee area) of £1,597.36. (Table 30)

1.20.4    The amount of 2026/27 Lloyd Square Garden Area Charge and the Basic Islington Council Tax for each valuation band. (Tables 31 and 32)

1.20.5    The total amount of 2026/27 council tax (including the GLA precept) for each valuation band over each of the council’s areas. (Tables 34 and 35)

1.21          To note the council’s estimated retained business rates funding in 2026/27, as per the 2026/27 NNDR1 return. (Paragraph 8.8 and Table 36).

1.22          To note the council’s forecast national non-domestic rates (NNDR) position for 2025/26. (Paragraph 8.9 and Table 37)

Matters to Consider in Setting the Budget (Section 9) 

1.23          To have regard to the Section 151 Officer’s report on the robustness of the estimates included in the budget and the adequacy of the proposed financial reserves when making decisions about the budget and the level of council tax, as required under Section 25(2) of the Local Government Act 2003. (Paragraphs 9.1-9.8) 

1.24          To note the Monitoring Officer comments. (Paragraphs 9.9-9.14)

1.25          To note the Environmental Implications and Contribution to Achieving a Net Zero Carbon Islington by 2030. (Paragraphs 9.15-9.25)

1.26          To note the Equality Impact Assessment and to take full account of it in approving the overall budget and related proposals. (Paragraphs 9.26-9.32)

1.27          To note that the council invited residents and NNDR payers (and representatives of NNDR payers) to comment on the draft 2026/27 budget proposals in this report, as required under Section 65 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992. (Paragraphs 9.33-9.39) 

1.28          To agree and recommend to Full Council the Supporting Small Business Relief Scheme for NNDR payers. (Paragraphs 9.40-9.46 and Appendix I1)

1.29          To agree and recommend to Full Council the Pubs and Live Music Venues Relief Scheme for NNDR payers. (Paragraphs 9.47-9.52 and Appendix I2)

1.30          To agree and recommend to Full Council the Pay Policy Statement for 2026/27. (Paragraphs 9.53-9.54 and Appendix H)

 

 

Decisions:

AGREED 1. Recommendations

2. Revised Appendix 2 relating to HRA Fees and Charges (amending the decision made by the Executive on 22 January 2026)

3. To note the observations of the Audit and Risk Committee and the Overview and Scrutiny Committee on the budget proposals

 

Reasons for the decision – as detailed in the report

Other options considered – as detailed in the report

Conflicts of interest / dispensations granted – none
