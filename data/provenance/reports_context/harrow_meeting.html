271.

Declarations of Interest

To receive declarations of disclosable pecuniary or non pecuniary interests, arising from business to be transacted at this meeting, from all Members of the Council.

Additional documents:

  • Webcast for 271.

Minutes:

The following interests were declared:-

 

Councillor Matthew Goodwin-Freeman declared a non-pecuniary interest in that he was employed by the Harrow East MP.  He would remain in the room.

 

Councillor Stephen Greek declared a pecuniary interest in that he was a Council appointed representative on the Harrow Strategic Development Partnership but that he had a dispensation to stay in the room to speak but not vote.

 

Councillor Kanti Rabadia declared a pecuniary interest in that he was a Council appointed representative on the Harrow Strategic Development Partnership but that he had a dispensation to stay in the room to speak but not vote.

 

The Monitoring Officer advised that Section 106 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 provided that any Member who had undischarged arrears of at least two months in respect of their Council Tax liability was precluded from participating in a vote on the setting of the Council Tax at Full Council.  If any Member believed themselves to be in that position, they must disclose the fact that this section applied to him/her and not vote on any question with respect to the matter.  Members were advised that it was a criminal offence if he/she failed to disclose and remained in the meeting and voted on the matter.

In accordance with Council Procedure Rule 6.2, the Mayor announced that an urgent tabled recommendation from the Chief Officers’ Employment Panel on the Employment arrangements for the Managing Director (Head of Paid Service) and an urgent tabled report, Approval of reason for absence of a Councillor, would be included on the business of the meeting and would taken after the conclusion of the budget debate and item 11, Capital Programme 2026-27 to 2028-29.  The recommendation, that had been made after the publication of the Summons, was urgent to clarify the employment arrangements of the Managing Director (Head of Paid Service).  The next meeting of Council was 20 May 2026.  The report was urgent as approval to the reason for absence was required before the end of the current dispensation.
