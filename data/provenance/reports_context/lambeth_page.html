Decision details

Statutory Consultation – Introduction of Permit Holder Only Past This Point (PHOPTP) permit parking in The Paddocks, Clapham Common as an extension to Clapham (Zone L) Controlled Parking Zone (CPZ).

  • Find out more about this issue

Decision Maker: Assistant Director - Parking, Network Management and Fleet

Decision status: Recommendations Approved

Is Key decision?: No

Is subject to call in?: No

Decision:

1.         Following consideration of representations received during statutory consultation detailed in this report, to make traffic management orders to introduce PHOPTP permit parking into “The Paddocks” as shown in the plan in Appendix A, as an extension of the existing Clapham (Zone L) CPZ.

 

2.         To exercise discretion not to hold a public inquiry pursuant to Regulation 9 of the Local Authorities Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1996.

 

3.         To allocate £6,000 from the unallocated Parking ‘theme’ budget in the Capital Investment Programme (CIP) approved by Cabinet in July 2020 to fund the implementation costs outlined in this report.

 

Publication date: 22/08/2022

Date of decision: 31/08/2022
