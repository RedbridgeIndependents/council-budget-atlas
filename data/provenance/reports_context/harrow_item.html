Agenda item

Revenue Budget 2026-27 and Medium Term Financial Strategy to 2028-29

  • Meeting of (Council Tax), Council, Thursday 26 February 2026 6.30 pm (Item 277.)

Recommendation:

Cabinet

 

(19 February 2026)

 

Minutes:

Upon the meeting moving to the vote on the Recommendation it was carried.  The voting was as follows:

 

In Favour of the Recommendation:  Councillors David Ashton, Marilyn Ashton, Christopher Baxter, June Baxter, Benjamin, Bharadia, Blackman, Chauhan, Chowdhury, Goodwin-Freeman, Greek, Halai, Hall, Hirani, Idaikkadar, Jogia, Karia, Kumaran, Lammiman, Moshenson, Mote, Osborn, Mina Parmar, Pritesh Patel, Rabadia, Stevenson, Sumaria, Teli and Wagman.

 

Against the Recommendation:  Councillors Ali, Anderson, Assad, Brown, Choudhury, Gilligan, Graham Henson, Maxine Henson, Hickman, Hussain, Miles, Parekh, Perry, Proctor, Shah-Levy, Krishna Suresh, Sasi Suresh and Weiss.

 

Abstain:  Her Worshipful the Mayor Councillor Anjana Patel

 

Absent:  Councillors Dolor, Kalu, Kulig, Mithani, O’Dell, Varsha Parmar and Rekha Shah.

 

RESOLVED:  That

 

(1)            the 2026/27 budget be approved, to enable the Council Tax for 2026/27 to be set;

 

(2)           the Model Council Tax Resolution 2026/27, as set out in Appendix 1 to these minutes, be approved and the Chief Finance Officer be authorised to make minor amendments to the resolution or amendments that may be required due to the GLA precept setting;

 

(3)           in accordance with Section 38 (2) of the Local Government Finance Act 1992, the Managing Director be instructed to place a notice in the local press of the amounts set under resolution 2 above within a period of 21 days following the Council’s decision;

 

(4)           the Medium-Term Financial Strategy, as set out in Appendix 2 to the officer’s report, be approved;

 

(5)           the current (2025/26) CTS Scheme be rolled over for the financial year 2026/27, as set out in paragraph 1.55 of the Cabinet report;

 

(6)           the sum of £100,000 for a Council Tax Hardship Fund be approved to be provided under Section 13A Local Government Finance Act 1992 for Council Tax Support claimants as set out in paragraph 1.56 of the Cabinet report;

 

(7)           the 2026/27 Annual Pay Policy Statement, as set out in appendix 13 to the Cabinet report, be approved;

 

(8)           the Capital Receipts Flexibility Strategy, as set out in appendix 14 to the Cabinet report, be approved.
