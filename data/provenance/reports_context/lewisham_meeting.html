Temporary changes to polling places for May 2026 and delegation to the Returning Officer to make future temporary amendments PDF 98 KB

Additional documents:

  • Appendix A - Maps of wards & new poroposed polling places , item 4. PDF 3 MB
  • Webcast for 4.

Decision:

It was MOVED by Councillor Wise, SECONDED by Councillor Walsh and unanimously RESOLVED that:

 

(i) the changes to the polling places for polling districts EHG2 (Hither Green), EBE2 (Bellingham) and WSY3 (Sydenham) for May 2026 be approved;

(ii) the revised polling scheme for May 2026 be approved;

(iii) authority be delegated to the Returning Officer to make temporary amendments to polling places if urgent issues arise and where there is not enough time for amendments to be made and approved by Council; and

(iv) officers be instructed to conduct a polling district review in Sydenham as soon as practicable after the May elections.
