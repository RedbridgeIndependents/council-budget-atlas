Addendum to Budget Report 2026-27 
 
On Monday 23 February 2026, the Council received confirmation that its application for 
Exceptional Financial Support (EFS) has been agreed in-principle. Lambeth is one of 35 
local authorities to be granted capitalisation support totalling £1.5bn for the next financial 
year and one of eight London boroughs.  
 
Details of the Council’s application are set out in the main report. The proposed budget for 
2026-27 has been drafted on the assumption that the application for EFS would be 
successful with the full cost taken into account in the Medium Term Financial Strategy 
(MTFS) so there is no change to the report as published.
