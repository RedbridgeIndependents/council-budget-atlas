2. Report Summary 
 
 
1. This report sets out Cabinet’s formal recommendations to Borough Council in 
respect of the General Fund revenue budget for 2026/27 and the resulting 
level of Council Tax, along with the recommended Housing Revenue Account 
(HRA) budget for 2026/27, the capital programme for 2026/27 to 2029/30, the 
updated Capital Strategy, fees and charges, the Treasury Management Annual 
Strategy, Annual Investment Strategy, Statement on Minimum Revenue 
Provision and Prudential and Treasury Indicators, arising from Cabinet’s 
budget decisions taken on 10 February 2026, based on the attached Cabinet 
report. 
 
2. The General Fund budget recommendations result in a Council Tax 
Requirement of £158.437m, leading to a Band D Council Tax of £1,675.05, an 
increase of 4.99%. The 2026/27 increase in the Council Tax is in two parts, 
firstly an Adult Social Care precept of a 2% increase over the Council Tax for 
2025/26, and secondly a general increase in Council Tax of 2.99%. 
 
3. The report’s recommendations include the formal statutory calculation required 
for setting the Council Tax for 2026/27, as set out in Sections 30, 31 and 36 of 
the Local Government Finance Act 1992. These recommendations are being 
made because under the council’s budget and policy framework, Borough 
Council is required to consider Cabinet’s budget recommendations and 
formally set the budget and Council Tax for 2026/27, in line with statutory 
requirements. 
 
4. Approval of the budgets, fees and charges and strategies in this report will 
allow the council to continue its ongoing operations and maintain internal 
financial controls throughout the 2026/27 financial year. Resident households 
will be individually notified of the increase in Council Tax in billing for 2026/27 
issued in March 2026. This will also include an expected increase in the 
Greater London Authority (GLA) precept, as set out in the attached report. 
 
5. The GLA precept and associated Council Tax recommendation is based on 
the draft consolidated budget released by the Mayor of London in January 
2026. Although not expected to change, the GLA precept will only be 
confirmed following the London Assembly meeting due to be held on 26 
February 2026.
